03 / The philosophy

Good materials deserve another chapter.

We believe craftsmanship can be both practical and playful. A board with character, a piece of old machinery, a weathered fixture: each carries possibilities worth exploring. We reclaim what we can, imagine what it might become, and make things meant to be used—not just admired.

Reclaimed wood. Real craftsmanship.

04 / How a commission begins

From idea to heirloom.

Custom work begins with a conversation, not a shopping cart.

I.

Tell the story

What do you need? Where will it live? Do you have cherished materials or an unusual idea worth saving?

II.

Find the form

We discuss the direction, practical details, materials, scope, and what a sensible build might involve.

III.

Make it matter

With an agreed plan, the idea becomes something useful, personal, and built to earn its place.

05 / Inside the workshop

Character comes standard.

Perfectly imperfect. Intentionally made.

Not every scar should be sanded away. Some tell you where a material has been—and why it deserves to be here. We look for that balance between rustic charm, sound construction, and genuine usefulness.

The workshop is growing, and so is the catalog. Follow along as ideas graduate from the sketchbook to real, working pieces.
